From anywhere

CMTeam Online

For athletes who train remotely and want the same follow-up as those in the group.

  • An individual plan built by San, published weekly on TrainingPeaks
  • Post-workout feedback read right away, with guidance and adjustments
  • Unlimited training adjustments, whenever your routine calls for it
  • Unlimited WhatsApp with Carla Moreno, Monday to Saturday, aligned with San on every change
  • Tests to set your training zones
  • An individual, detailed race plan: a complete document built for your race
  • CMTeam Talks: technical learning sessions
  • Discounts and special service with the team’s partners
  • Exclusive group with new content every week

CMTeam Miami

For athletes in Miami who want to train with the team, plus all the online coaching.

  • Everything in CMTeam Online
  • Direct contact with your coaches, San and Carla, 4 times a week
  • Tuesday and Thursday: run and swim with the group

  • Saturday: long run and open-water swim
  • Brick and race simulation every month at Tribeach
  • At year-end: the team kit and the season’s book
