Release of Liability, Waiver and Assumption of Risk

1. PARTIES AND SCOPE

I, the undersigned Athlete, am voluntarily participating in training, coaching, group sessions, physical activities, and related services provided by CMTeam Consulting LLC (“CMTeam”), operated by San Palma and Carla Moreno, based in Miami, State of Florida, United States of America.

In this Agreement, the “Released Parties” means CMTeam Consulting LLC, San Palma, and Carla Moreno.

This Agreement applies to every CMTeam activity in which I participate, in person or remotely, from the date of my signature until I revoke it in writing.

2. ASSUMPTION OF RISK

I understand that endurance sports training, including running, trail running, triathlon, swimming, open-water swimming, cycling, strength training, and related activities, involves inherent risks that cannot be eliminated.

These risks include, but are not limited to:

  • physical injury, muscle strains, joint injuries, fractures, illness, or overuse injuries;
  • dehydration, heat exhaustion, heat stroke, sunburn, or hypothermia;
  • cardiovascular events, including cardiac arrest, and other medical emergencies;
  • falls, collisions, or accidents, including with motor vehicles, cyclists, pedestrians, or other athletes during group sessions;
  • training on public roads, bridges, sidewalks, trails, and parks with traffic, uneven surfaces, debris, and low visibility, including early-morning or night sessions;
  • open-water risks such as currents, waves, boats and watercraft, marine life, poor water quality, and drowning;
  • weather conditions, including extreme heat and humidity, lightning, storms, and sudden changes in conditions;
  • failure or misuse of my own equipment, such as bicycles, helmets, wetsuits, or devices.

I understand that sessions take place in public locations and facilities that are not owned, operated, or controlled by CMTeam.

I knowingly and freely assume all risks, both known and unknown, associated with my participation.

3. MEDICAL RESPONSIBILITY

I affirm that:

  • I am physically and medically able to participate in training activities.
  • I will inform CMTeam of any medical condition, injury, medication, or limitation that may affect my safety, and of any change to my health.
  • I understand that CMTeam, San Palma, and Carla Moreno are not medical professionals.
  • I am solely responsible for seeking medical clearance and professional advice when appropriate.

I understand that training guidance, nutritional suggestions, hydration strategies, or supplementation discussions are not medical or nutritional prescriptions, and do not replace consultation with a licensed physician or registered dietitian.

4. EMERGENCY MEDICAL TREATMENT

In the event of an injury or medical emergency, I authorize CMTeam to call emergency services and to arrange medical care on my behalf if I am unable to do so. I understand that CMTeam is not obligated to provide medical care, and that I am responsible for all costs of any emergency transport and medical treatment I receive.

5. ATHLETE RESPONSIBILITIES

During CMTeam activities, I agree to:

  • follow all traffic laws, and wear an approved helmet at all times while cycling;
  • use front and rear lights on my bicycle in low-light conditions;
  • swim within my ability in open water, and use a brightly colored cap and a safety buoy when recommended;
  • keep my equipment in safe working condition;
  • carry identification, hydration, and any personal medication I may need;
  • follow the coaches’ instructions, and stop immediately and inform a coach if I feel unwell.

6. RELEASE AND WAIVER OF LIABILITY

I hereby release, waive, and discharge the Released Parties from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action arising from injury, illness, death, damage, or loss that may occur as a result of my participation in training, coaching, or related activities.

This release applies to claims arising from the negligence of the Released Parties or from any other cause, to the fullest extent permitted by law. It is binding on me, my heirs, executors, and assigns.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any claims, losses, or expenses, including reasonable attorney fees, arising from my own actions or conduct during CMTeam activities.

8. NO GUARANTEE OF RESULTS

I understand that athletic performance outcomes cannot be guaranteed. Training plans and coaching guidance are designed to support development, safety, and progression, but results depend on multiple factors beyond the control of CMTeam.

9. PHOTO / MEDIA AUTHORIZATION

I grant permission for CMTeam to use photos, videos, or training-related media involving my participation for educational or promotional purposes, unless I notify CMTeam otherwise in writing.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, United States of America, without regard to conflict of law principles.

Any legal action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the courts of Miami-Dade County, Florida.

11. SEVERABILITY AND ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE

If any part of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

I agree that my electronic signature, including a typed name submitted through an online form, has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.

12. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

I confirm that I am at least 18 years old, that I have read and fully understand this Agreement, and that I am giving up substantial legal rights, including the right to sue the Released Parties. I sign it voluntarily and acknowledge that it is legally binding.
